## Product and purpose

BAIRA GPT is an experimental scientific research assistant intended to aid evidence discovery, numerical analysis, simulation, Monte Carlo experiments, and reporting. Outputs are AI-generated scientific results rather than independently verified empirical observations.

## Scientific limitations

Users must independently verify data provenance, calculations, scientific interpretations, model assumptions, numerical precision, units, applicable physical regimes and conclusions before relying on an output for research or high-stakes decisions. BAIRA GPT is not a general guarantee of accurate physics, real-world prediction, safe engineering design or independent experimental confirmation.

Some features require Python and file-generation tools supplied by the host; they may not be available in every ChatGPT session. No continuous sensor monitoring, unrestricted autonomy, or access to unauthorized tools is promised.
