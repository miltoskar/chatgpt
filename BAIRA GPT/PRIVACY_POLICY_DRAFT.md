# BAIRA GPT — Privacy policy draft (not yet approved)

**Status:** Draft for review by the publisher. This is **not** a published final privacy policy and must not yet be used as the policy URL in an OpenAI plugin submission.

## Scope and known technical architecture

BAIRA GPT is a research-oriented ChatGPT skill package with local Python modules. The public submission candidate does not declare its own remote MCP server or a BAIRA-operated online account system. Its tools, when invoked, execute only through capabilities made available and authorized by the ChatGPT host.

A research interaction may involve prompts, uploaded research datasets, published scientific sources, simulation parameters, generated outputs, and exports. ChatGPT and any explicitly connected third-party services process data under their own privacy policies and account settings. BAIRA GPT must not imply it can control those services' retention or data-use practices.
